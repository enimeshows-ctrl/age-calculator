About Age Calculator

This free Age Calculator helps you calculate your exact age from your date of birth. It shows your age in years, months and days, along with your total age in months, weeks and days.

You can also find out how many days are left until your next birthday. Simply enter your date of birth and click the Calculate Age button.

How to Calculate Your Age

Enter your date of birth, and the calculator compares it with today's date to calculate your exact age. The result includes years, months, days and your next birthday information.
