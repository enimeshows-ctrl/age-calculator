About GST Calculator

This free GST Calculator helps you calculate GST amount, CGST, SGST and the final price quickly. Enter the amount, select the GST rate and choose whether GST should be added or removed from the amount.

The calculator supports common GST rates such as 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%. For GST calculations in India, the GST amount is divided equally into CGST and SGST when applicable.

How to Calculate GST

Enter the original amount, select the GST rate and choose the calculation type. The calculator will show the GST amount, CGST, SGST and final amount instantly.
